# 衣橱插画生成记录

使用 Codex 的内建 ImageGen，以项目的三个角色原画作为风格参考。每张提交图分成六件独立单品，经透明边缘清理和定位后导出为 RGBA PNG。


## 上衣第一组

```text
Use case: illustration-story. An asset atlas of six individual garments for a refined chibi anime dress-up game. Attached original character art is REFERENCE for linework, shading and cloth details only. Match the beautiful original illustration: deep purple-black tapered inking, warm reflected light, layered cel shadows, tailored dimensional cloth folds at seams, carefully designed metalwork and embroidered details, restrained tiny accents, shape that looks professionally hand drawn. Garments are from a cozy fantasy Moon Teahouse with small moon and tea motifs, and should be individually beautiful at a small size. VERY STRICT placement: wide 3:2 landscape canvas, exactly 3 evenly spaced columns and 2 evenly spaced rows, precisely one complete isolated garment centered in each equal cell. Each fills at most 82% width and 80% height of its cell. At least 35px transparent gap between pictures and clear edge margins. No overlap, no extras, no labels, no text, no stamps, no mannequins, no body parts, no hands, no neck skin, no shadows on backdrop, no ground, no decorative floating objects, no background color or checkerboard. TRUE transparent RGBA background. Exactly straight-on symmetrical frontal game apparel except where a specific fashion cut is asymmetric. Draw each item as a fitted paper doll cutout, no angled perspective. Give each the same garment scale. Clear outline and substantial clean details; not simplistic vector graphic. Order is strict left to right, top then bottom.
Atlas content is TOPS ONLY, cropped at natural waist, worn by chibis with fairly short torsos and broad shoulders. All six garments consist of the front torso AND its own sleeves, sleeves pointing DIAGONALLY OUT AND DOWN in a clear A pose about 30 degrees out from vertical; leave a clear transparent triangular gap under each arm so sleeves can be cut out and posed separately. No garment hangs far below the waist. Top row: (1) Pearl lilac moon-petal blouse, navy silk scalloped bib collar, puff sleeves, tea-stained lace edging, small apricot bow and gold moon pin; (2) Lagoon-blue sailor blouse with a short puff sleeve, ivory double-stripe sailor collar and coral cravat, little enamel shell button; (3) Rust leather cropped rider jacket, long articulated sleeves, crossed blackened-brass zipper, lapels, rivets, stitching and subtle flame-colored elbow panels. Bottom row: (4) Moss teahouse hoodie with long roomy sleeves, kangaroo pocket with leaf embroidery, drawstrings and ribbed cuffs, sage-green hood lining; (5) Plum wrap kimono top with long wide cuff sleeves, woven piping, warm sakura embroidered on one chest panel, wine waist ties; (6) Berry varsity jacket, warm cream long sleeves, stitched salmon star patch, pearl snaps and striped ribbed waistband. Each deliberately different silhouette and fashion detail.
```
